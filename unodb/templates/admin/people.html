Rename a player or location (typos, a new spelling). The old name is kept in the audit log. Two players can't share a name, and the player's page address (/player/joe-miller) follows the name.

Merging players (the same person entered under two names) can't be done on this page, on purpose: it can't be undone from here. On the server run podman exec unodb flask --app unodb uno merge-players "Joe" "Joe Miller" – all games of the first player move to the second and the first name is removed. It makes a backup first and writes an audit log entry.
