Ratings

The rating is how many points per game a player does better than the others at the same table (fewer points is better, so +2 means 2 points per game fewer than the others). Black-card games count half, since their scores are doubled.

A single game is mostly luck: points vary by about ±30 from game to game, while the real differences between players are only a few points. So the rating doesn't take the plain average: it pulls the average toward 0 the fewer games someone has, because over a few games chance alone gives big values. With hundreds of games the rating gets close to the average.
