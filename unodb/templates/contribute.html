Add your own games

Do you keep score when you play UNO? You can add your games to this database and get the same statistics and charts for your group.

How to get a login

    {# email_off: Cloudflare would replace the address with a version that needs its inline decoder script, which the CSP blocks #}
  1. Send an email to arun@nubati.net and tell me a bit about your group.
  2. You get a link back. It works once and only for a short time.
  3. Open the link and set up a passkey: your phone or computer saves it, and you unlock it with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. There is no password to remember.
  4. Log in with the passkey and enter your scores.

Before you enter games

  • Player names: use first and last name (e.g. "Joe Miller"), so your players don't get mixed up with players of the same first name who are already in the database.
  • Game type: house rules differ. If yours differ from the existing game types, create your own and describe the rules.
  • Your sessions count in the statistics right away. You can correct or delete the sessions you entered at any time.

Entering games is not open yet; this part of the site is still being built.
